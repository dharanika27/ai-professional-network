# E8-S5 — Dashboard hub, Job Matching, Job Details, Settings screens

**Layer:** UI
**Group:** G
**Depends on:** E6-S4, E7-S3, E8-S1

## Description

The Dashboard hub surfacing profile completion, recent AI analyses, matched jobs, and
recommended next actions; the Job Matching results screen with fit/gap explanations; Job
Details; and Settings including data deletion.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The Dashboard surfaces profile completion, recent AI analyses, matched jobs, and recommended next actions, each linking to its respective screen.
2. The Job Matching screen calls `POST /api/jobs/match` and renders ranked jobs with fit score, fit explanation, and gaps per job.
3. Selecting a matched job opens Job Details via `GET /api/jobs/{id}` showing full posting information.
4. Settings allows the user to delete their resume and associated AI analyses (calling `DELETE /api/resume/{id}`) with a confirmation step, and to toggle the theme.
5. All screens render correctly in light and dark themes, at mobile/tablet/desktop breakpoints, and are keyboard navigable to WCAG 2.1 AA.
